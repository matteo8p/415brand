# Posts to ignore: Goody

Filtering is silent. Dropped posts are not shown to the reader. This file is the only record, so keep it current.

These are research415's proposed rules, carried over from the Cloudless and Ref lists and adapted for a corporate-gifting market. **None have been confirmed by Goody yet.** Ask in the first working session which to keep.

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bare-word noise, both names.** `goody` meaning goody bag, goody two-shoes, Goody Products Inc. (hair accessories), or the surname. `snappy` meaning quick, Snappy Shopper, Snappy Tots, or Google's Snappy compression library. | Search artefact. See the name problem in `CLIENT.md`. This will be the single largest category of dropped rows |
| 2 | **Consumer gift-idea content.** "Best gifts for dad," listicles, affiliate gift guides, Etsy sellers, personal Secret Santa threads. | Enormous volume, zero business intent. Exception: a thread about a *company* gift someone received is recipient-sentiment signal and belongs in a bullet |
| 3 | **Recipients asking how to redeem or whether it's a scam.** "What is a Snappy Gift, is it a scam?" and equivalents. | Not buyers. **But count them** — the volume is itself a finding about a competitor's recipient experience, and it belongs in a bullet, never as a lead row |
| 4 | **Vendor content marketing.** Sendoso, Reachdesk, SnackNation, TeamSunday, Docket and the rest ranking each other in "top 10 platforms" posts. | Not people. Log a genuinely new competitive claim in `research/competitors.md` instead. Note that **Goody does this too** — do not treat its own blog as third-party evidence |
| 5 | **Promotional-products / ASI trade press.** Swag industry newsletters, imprint trade shows, distributor announcements. | Adjacent industry, wrong buyer. Exception: a named Covver/Snappy integration development |
| 6 | **Already building their own.** Anyone shipping a competing gifting or recognition platform. | Competitor, not customer. Log in `research/competitors.md` |
| 7 | **Affiliate and launch spam.** Discount-code bots, "best deals" accounts, Product Hunt amplification. | Not people |
| 8 | **Astroturf.** Templated recommendation posts from new accounts in buying threads. Cloudless's Willow case is the reference pattern. | Log the campaign once in a bullet. Never list the posts as mentions. **Watch for this specifically in gifting-platform recommendation threads** — the incentive is obvious and at least one Goody employee has self-identified while recommending Goody, which is honest but is not organic mention volume and must not be counted as one |
| 9 | **Enterprise procurement-cycle orgs.** Fortune 100 platform and workplace teams. | Snappy has 56% of them locked and Goody has no verified SOC 2. Revisit once question 3 in `CLIENT.md` is answered |
| 10 | **Warehousing, bulk-kit and event-logistics asks.** "I need 2,000 kits stored and shipped from a warehouse." | Goody does not do this and says so. Sending these wastes the client's time |
