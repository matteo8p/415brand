# Posts to ignore

Tim's standing list of post types that are not worth a reply, built up over time.
Every daily report is filtered through this, and the report shows what got dropped
and why so Tim can correct the list.

Source of the original request: `TIMS_PREFERENCES.md` (Aug 27, 2026) —
> "when I was doing this myself, I had a way to leave feedback on posts sourced. So for
> example, a lot of these posts are saying 'i just subscribed to Wispr Flow'. Doesn't make
> sense to put a free version in front of that guy. Or posts about free competitors on
> Reddit. So I had a list of rules that I had made for posts to ignore that I built up over time."

## Confirmed by Tim

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Just subscribed / just renewed and happy.** "I just subscribed", "best $15/mo I spend", "paid for the year and love it". | They have just chosen to pay. A free alternative is not a pitch, it is an argument. |
| 2 | **Posts about free competitors.** Open-source clones, Handy, FluidVoice, OpenTypeless, Rhino Voice, "why pay when this is free". | The audience already has a free option, so "free" is not a wedge. Cloudless has to win on polish, not price, and that is a different conversation. |

### Rule 1 exception — angry payers
Someone who paid *and* is now asking for a refund or saying quality collapsed is a churn
target, not a fresh subscriber. Those stay in the report. The test is the sentiment, not
the transaction.

## Proposed (added by 415brand, awaiting Tim's yes/no)

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Subscription resale and deal spam.** r/IndiaDealsExchange, r/Discount_Subscription, r/discouthub, "[H] Wispr Flow Pro 10 months [W] ₹950 UPI". | Resellers, not users. |
| 4 | **Affiliate and referral posts.** Anything carrying a `wisprflow.ai/r?WISPR…` code or `#affiliate`. | Paid promoters. Replying argues with someone's commission. |
| 5 | **Astroturf.** Templated posts from throwaway accounts — currently Willow's "I started researching…" format. | Not real people. Log the campaign, do not reply to it. |
| 6 | **Linux-only users.** "wispr flow on linux", "arch btw". | Cloudless has no Linux build. Handy wins there and we say so. |
| 7 | **Off-topic search noise.** Foreign-language threads and posts where Wispr appears only in a long tool list. | Search artefacts. |
