# Posts to ignore: Adapt

Filtering is silent. Dropped posts are not shown to the reader. This file is the only record, so keep it current.

These are research415's proposed rules, derived from the Viktor research and the Cloudless list. **None have been confirmed by Adapt yet.** Ask in the next working session which to keep.

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bare-word noise.** Any hit on `viktor` that is a person's first name, a football club, a film character, or an unrelated product. | Search artefact. Never search the bare word. See the name collisions note in `CLIENT.md`. |
| 2 | **Viktor's own staff and paid creators.** Anyone whose bio lists Zeta AI or Viktor, and any post carrying a Viktor referral link or a paid-partnership label. | Not a lead. Paid creators are logged in the advertising section as evidence of spend, never listed as organic mentions. |
| 3 | **Launch-day amplification.** The group of big accounts that reply to each other's Viktor posts on launch day. | Not organic. Count the pattern once in the advertising section, do not list the posts. |
| 4 | **Astroturf.** Templated posts from new accounts. Cloudless's Willow case is the reference pattern. | Log the campaign once in a bullet. Never list the posts as mentions. |
| 5 | **Already building their own.** Anyone shipping a competing Slack agent or AI coworker. | Competitor, not customer. Log them in `research/viktor.md` or a competitors note instead. |
| 6 | **Pure model chatter.** Model releases and benchmark threads with no workflow pain. | Wrong layer. Adapt sells the layer above the model. |
