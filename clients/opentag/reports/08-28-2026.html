3What happened in the category

  • OpenTag's own launch post is the warmest lead source you have. @kamikariat announced OpenTag on Aug 26 and it took 106 replies, 346 likes and 31 reposts. Nothing else in this brief comes close. Work that reply tree before anything else here, and tell us if you want it pulled and sorted tomorrow.
  • A direct competitor launched yesterday. AgentConnect announced itself as "the open-source, self-hosted, multi-agent alternative to Claude Tag" (45 likes, 15 replies). It takes the same anti-lock-in position OpenTag takes, from the open-source side.
  • That launch was amplified by about ten accounts within six hours, all using near-identical framing about agent handoff (111 likes, 89, 60, and more in Spanish and Hindi). Those numbers were bought, not earned. The launch is real. The excitement is not.
  • Salesforce and Anthropic announced Claudeforce on Aug 26: a Salesforce plugin inside Claude, Claude CoWork, and 37 pre-built sales skills. Anthropic is moving further into the surface OpenTag sells into, not away from it.
  • Anthropic updated Claude in Slack so it reads a whole channel and decides whether to reply, start a thread, route the work, or stay quiet. Claude Tag is getting more ambient, which makes the per-message bill less predictable. That helps OpenTag.
  • @raphaelschaad pitched OpenTag publicly on Aug 26 (54 likes, 6 reposts): "Tag is restricted to Anthropic's models and expensive. Meet OpenTag: model-agnostic (100 choices) and 10x cheaper." He says 100 models and 10x. The site says 80+ and 70%. Pick one number and give it to advocates, because right now the loudest public claim about OpenTag is one nobody at OpenTag wrote.
