1. **Dictation (rows 1–20).** Cloudless is a paying client in this category and `clients/cloudless/IGNORE_RULES.md` flags Willow Voice for astroturfing. Sending a public URL is defensible; **signing** Aqua, superwhisper or Typeless without telling Tim first is not.
2. **Gifting (rows 21–30).** Goody is an unsigned prospect here and has never been contacted. Open Goody first, or accept this category is a race and pitch the field.
3. **Gateway (rows 31–40).** Experiential Labs is an unsigned prospect and the OpenRouter sample names them on the public site. Requesty and Respan are their direct rivals.
4. **Slack agents (rows 41–49).** Adapt and OpenTag are both live, and `opentag/COMPETITORS.md` already carries an Adapt/OpenTag conflict notice.
5. **`/samples/wispr` is publicly addressed "For the Willow Voice team."** Willow has no folder in `clients/`, so it looks like they were never contacted — while Cloudless, a paying client, has Willow flagged for astroturfing and runs a `/willow-voice-alternative` page. Every dictation founder we email will open that sample and see us apparently working for Willow. Anonymize it the way `wispr-daily` was, or be ready to explain it.
